## Navigator bar (2026 Horizon OS)

Bottom glass bar tabs:

1. You
2. Notifications
3. People
4. App Library
5. Quick Controls
6. Show / hide windows
7. Passthrough / VR Home

Toggle **Quest** vs **Orion** in the top corner. Press the blue Meta button or key `M` to show or hide the overlay.
